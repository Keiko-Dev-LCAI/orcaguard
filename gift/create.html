How this works, and the limits

Instructions

  • You send the link yourself. The app never sees your email or the gift code.
  • Anyone who opens the link can claim the card. Keep amounts small.
  • After the end date the card can only be returned to the sender. Early cancel is possible only before it is claimed.
  • The recipient needs an address they control, or must save a page-generated key before the coins move.

Privacy

We keep no personal information. No accounts, no database, and no copy of your email, the gift code, or your visit. The one-time key lives only in the link you send. A hosted helper may submit the redeem for you and sees only what is needed to do that. It stores nothing.
